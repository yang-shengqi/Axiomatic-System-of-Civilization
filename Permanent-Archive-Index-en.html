This system, constructed by independent scholar Yang Shengqi, provides a complete closed‑loop framework for the state of civilization — from axiomatic definitions through diagnosis, measurement, monitoring, prescription, operations, and validation.
It comprises eleven core documents organised into seven logical layers (Meta‑Framework → Diagnosis → Measurement → Monitoring → Prescription → Operations → Validation), and establishes three meta‑principles:

Foundational Veto
Bottom lines are non‑negotiable; any system violating civilisational bottom lines must not be adopted.
Primacy of Lived Experience
Actual human experience takes precedence over abstract statistics — data can be embellished, but people's lived feelings do not lie.
Law of the Shortest Plank
The condition of the most vulnerable determines the overall floor — the true level of civilisation is measured by its shortest plank.
Civilisation Index = (Livelihood & Labour Security + Equality of Labour Value + Fair Income Distribution + Freedom of Expression & Spirit) × 2.5
