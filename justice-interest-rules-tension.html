THE MISALIGNMENT OF JUSTICE, INTERESTS, AND RULES: A TENSION ANALYSIS OF SYSTEM SELF‑PRESERVATION AND EXTERNAL CORRECTION

正义、利益与规则的错位：系统自我维护与外部纠偏的张力分析

张力分析
ORCID: 0009-0008-3755-8960 全球首发: uncienine.wordpress.com 封装完成: 2026年7月

完整概叙 Overview

社会基石的核心判断是：正义是底层百姓最后的压舱石，利益是得利者的压舱石。当两者碰撞时，底层用直觉报警，而得利者用规则免责。The core judgment of the Foundation of Society is: justice is the last ballast for ordinary people; interests are the ballast for the advantaged. When they collide, ordinary people sound the alarm through intuition, while the advantaged use rules to immunize themselves.

任何体系，无论其宣称的目标多么崇高，最终的合法性都落在每一个具体个体所承受的实际体验上。体感胜于一切庞大叙事。Any system, regardless of how noble its proclaimed goals, ultimately derives its legitimacy from the actual experience borne by every concrete individual. Lived experience transcends all grand narratives.

凡是权力高度集中且缺乏有效外部制衡的系统，其“自我纠偏”的驱动力必然弱于“自我维护”的惯性。需要外力打破平衡，但外力是否有效，取决于它能否转化为制度内生的纠偏机制，而非仅仅是一次可以被消化的事件。In any system where power is highly concentrated and lacks effective external checks and balances, the driving force for "self‑correction" will inevitably be weaker than the inertia of "self‑preservation." External forces are required to break the equilibrium—but whether they are effective depends on whether they can be transformed into institutionally endogenous correction mechanisms, rather than merely being an event that can be absorbed and digested.

社会基石 The Foundation of Society

文明以自由为根基，自由以规则为边界，规则以公平、公正、公开为准绳。
Civilization is rooted in freedom; freedom is bounded by rules; rules are guided by fairness, justice, and openness.

当下情况 The Current Situation

正义成为了底层百姓最后的压舱石，利益成为了得利者的压舱石。
Justice has become the last ballast for ordinary people; interests have become the ballast for the advantaged.

最终结果 The Final Result

当两块石头碰撞时，底层百姓用直觉报警，而得利者用规则免责。
When the two ballasts collide, ordinary people sound the alarm through intuition, while the advantaged use rules to immunize themselves.

体感胜于一切庞大叙事 Lived Experience Transcends All Grand Narratives

任何体系，无论其宣称的目标多么崇高，最终的合法性都落在每一个具体个体所承受的实际体验上。
Any system, regardless of how noble its proclaimed goals, ultimately derives its legitimacy from the actual experience borne by every concrete individual.

系统的自我维护惯性 The Inertia of System Self‑Preservation

凡是权力高度集中且缺乏有效外部制衡的系统，其“自我纠偏”的驱动力必然弱于“自我维护”的惯性。
In any system where power is highly concentrated and lacks effective external checks and balances, the driving force for "self‑correction" will inevitably be weaker than the inertia of "self‑preservation."

这不是道德判断，而是系统论的基本常识。需要外力（比如危机、舆论、或内部的代际更替）来打破平衡。
This is not a moral judgment—it is basic systems theory. External forces (such as crises, public opinion, or internal generational succession) are required to break the equilibrium.

外力能否真正改变平衡？ Can External Forces Truly Shift the Equilibrium?

取决于它是否转化为制度内生的纠偏机制，而非仅仅是一次可以被消化的事件。
It depends on whether they can be transformed into institutionally endogenous correction mechanisms—rather than merely being an event that can be absorbed and digested.

核心判断 Core Judgment

系统自我维护的惯性，只有在外部冲击转化为制度内生的纠偏机制时，才可能被打破。否则，每一次危机都只是被消化，系统回到原来的轨道。
The inertia of system self‑preservation can only be broken when external shocks are transformed into institutionally endogenous correction mechanisms. Otherwise, every crisis is merely absorbed, and the system returns to its original trajectory.
