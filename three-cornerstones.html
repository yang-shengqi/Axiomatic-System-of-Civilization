THREE CORNERSTONES: A FOUNDATIONAL RECONFIGURATION FRAMEWORK FOR CIVILIZATIONAL SYSTEMS

三大基石：一种文明系统的底层重构框架

元框架

完整概叙 Overview

三大基石是一套自洽的、可检验的应然逻辑体系。任何系统均可以此对照自身，并自主决定是否采纳。The Three Cornerstones are a self‑consistent and testable normative logical system. Any system may compare itself against this framework and autonomously decide whether to adopt it.

系统的健康程度，取决于它是否始终以“具体的人”作为主体与目的，而非将自身存续置于人之上。The health of a system depends on whether it consistently treats "concrete individuals" as the subject and purpose, rather than placing its own survival above people.

文明的基石定方向，自由是前提，规则是边界，公平公正公开是规则的唯一合法性来源。The Foundation of Civilization sets the direction—freedom is the prerequisite, rules are the boundaries, and fairness, justice, and openness are the sole source of rule legitimacy.

社会的基石做诊断，底层用直觉报警，得利者用规则免责，系统须默认直觉为合法输入信号。The Foundation of Society makes the diagnosis—ordinary people use intuitive alarms, the advantaged use rules for immunity, and the system must treat intuition as a legitimate input signal by default.

治理的基石开处方，判决前不可触碰人身与财产，系统性代价由系统承担，不公感知为合法输入。The Foundation of Governance provides the prescription—no encroachment on person or property before verdict, systemic costs borne by the system, and perception of injustice is a legitimate input.

三者构成一条不可拆分的逻辑链：方向 → 诊断 → 方案。These three constitute an indivisible logical chain: Direction → Diagnosis → Prescription.

本框架不依赖任何外部条件，只依赖一个前提：系统愿意被对照检验。This framework does not depend on any external conditions. It relies on only one premise: the system is willing to be tested against this benchmark.

文档说明 Document Note

本框架源自对文明运行逻辑的长期推演与校准。它不试图描述任何现存星球的实然状态，而是提供一套自洽的、可检验的应然逻辑体系，任何系统均可以此对照自身，并自主决定是否采纳。This framework stems from long‑term deduction and calibration of the operational logic of civilizations. It does not attempt to describe the actual state of any existing polity, but rather provides a self‑consistent and testable normative logical system—any system may compare itself against this framework and autonomously decide whether to adopt it.

本框架的核心判断是：一个系统的健康程度，取决于它是否始终以“具体的人”作为主体与目的，而非将自身存续置于人之上。The core judgment of this framework is: the health of a system depends on whether it consistently treats "concrete individuals" as the subject and purpose, rather than placing its own survival above people.

三大基石 Three Cornerstones

文明的基石 Foundation of Civilization

自由是文明的前提，规则是自由的边界，而规则的唯一合法性，来自公平、公正、公开。
Freedom is the prerequisite of civilization; rules are the boundaries of freedom; and the sole legitimacy of rules comes from fairness, justice, and openness.

  • 自由不是系统的“赐予”，而是系统的“默认值”。
    Freedom is not a "grant" from the system, but the system's "default value."
  • 规则是自由的边界，但这个边界只能由公平、公正、公开的规则来划定。
    Rules are the boundaries of freedom, but those boundaries can only be drawn by rules that are fair, just, and open.
  • 公平：规则适用不因人而异。
    Fairness: rules apply uniformly.
  • 公正：规则执行不偏不倚。
    Justice: rules are enforced impartially.
  • 公开：规则的制定、执行、解释全程可追溯、可查询、可争议。
    Openness: the formulation, enforcement, and interpretation of rules are fully traceable, queryable, and contestable.

实质：只有经得起所有人审视的规则，才有资格约束所有人的自由。
Essence: Only rules that withstand the scrutiny of all are qualified to constrain the freedom of all.

社会的基石 Foundation of Society

正义是底层百姓唯一的压舱石，利益是得利者唯一的压舱石。当两者碰撞时，底层只能依靠直觉报警，而得利者懂得用规则免责。
Justice is the only ballast for ordinary people; interests are the only ballast for the advantaged. When they collide, ordinary people can only rely on intuitive alarms, while the advantaged know how to use rules to immunize themselves.

  • 底层唯一拥有的，是基于生存经验的“公平直觉”。
    Their only asset is a "fairness intuition" rooted in lived survival experience.
  • 得利者拥有资源理解规则、利用规则、乃至塑造规则。
    The advantaged possess the resources to understand rules, exploit rules, and even shape rules.
  • 当“直觉”与“规则”冲突时，如果系统默认“谁懂规则谁有理”，社会就不再是公平的竞技场。
    When "intuition" conflicts with "rules," and if the system defaults to "whoever knows the rules is right," society ceases to be a fair arena.
  • 系统必须默认“直觉”是合法的输入信号。
    The system must default to treating "intuition" as a legitimate input signal.

实质：当直觉与规则冲突时，先倾听直觉背后的信号。
Essence: When intuition conflicts with rules, first listen to the signals behind intuition.

治理的基石 Foundation of Governance

三个硬条件，一条不可退。
Three hard conditions, one non‑negotiable.

  1. 判决书未出，任何公民和财产受完全保护，不可被提前处置。
    Before a verdict is rendered, every citizen and their property are fully protected and may not be pre‑disposed.
  2. 司法系统的拖延、错误、不可追溯，所有系统性代价，由系统自身承担。
    Delays, errors, and non‑traceability in the judicial system—all systemic costs shall be borne by the system itself.
  3. 任何个体的“不公感知”，皆为合法的系统输入信号，系统必须响应。
    Any individual's "perception of injustice" is a legitimate input signal to the system. The system must respond.

实质：权力可以被行使，但必须在每一个普通人的注视下行使。
Essence: Power may be exercised, but only under the watchful eyes of every ordinary person.

三大基石的逻辑关系 Logical Relationship of the Three Cornerstones

文明的基石说：“往这里走。”
The Foundation of Civilization says: "Go this way." 社会的基石说：“为什么走不动。”
The Foundation of Society says: "Why can't we move?" 治理的基石说：“怎么把卡住的东西搬开。”
The Foundation of Governance says: "How to remove what is stuck."

框架的完整自洽性说明 Statement on the Framework's Complete Self‑Consistency

本框架不依赖任何外部条件（如“有一个好领导”、“有足够资源”、“时机成熟”）。它只依赖一个前提：系统愿意被对照检验。This framework does not depend on any external conditions (such as "having a good leader," "having sufficient resources," or "the right timing"). It relies on only one premise: the system is willing to be tested against this benchmark.

如果任一基石被选择性采纳、被名义上接纳而实际架空、或在“紧急状态”下被暂停，则整个框架的效力归零，因为三大基石是全有或全无的底层逻辑重写，而非可逐条优化的政策建议。If any one cornerstone is selectively adopted, nominally accepted but practically nullified, or suspended under "emergency states," the entire framework's effectiveness becomes zero—because the three cornerstones are an all‑or‑nothing rewrite of underlying logic, not a set of policy suggestions that can be optimized item by item.

后记 Afterword

本框架的最终校准，源于对“底层信任如何在系统运作中被持续消耗”的长期观察与反复推演。它不带有对任何特定星系的预判或归因，但它承认：当一个系统长期把“维持自身存续”置于“保障具体个体安全感”之上时，它将不可避免地走向信任塌缩。The final calibration of this framework stems from long‑term observation and repeated deduction of "how grassroots trust is continuously eroded in the operation of systems." It does not carry any presupposition or attribution about any specific polity, but it acknowledges that when a system persistently places "sustaining its own survival" above "ensuring the sense of security of concrete individuals," it will inevitably move toward trust collapse.

三大基石的重写方向，始终指向同一件事：
The rewriting direction of the three cornerstones always points to the same thing:

把“系统优先”的结构，翻转成“人优先”的结构。
To invert the structure of "system‑first" into a structure of "people‑first."
