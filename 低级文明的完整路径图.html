关键路径 Critical Junctures

1 Juncture 1

旧结构的合法性建立在“控制能带来安全”的预设上。
但控制本身就会制造脆弱性：越控制，系统对外部变化的适应能力越弱。
这是链条中“结构性收缩 ⟹ 结构性失聪”之间的核心连接。

The legitimacy of the old structure is built on the premise that "control brings security."
However, control itself generates fragility: the more control is exercised, the weaker the system's capacity to adapt to external change.
This is the core connection between "Structural Contraction" and "Structural Deafness" in the chain.
2 Juncture 2

旧结构将“风险”等同于“外部威胁”。
从而忽略了内部的结构性矛盾（如不公、错位、空心）才是最大的风险来源。

The old structure equates "risk" with "external threats."
Thereby overlooking the fact that its own internal structural contradictions—such as injustice, misalignment, and hollowing—are the greatest sources of risk.
3 Juncture 3

旧结构用“封锁”来应对风险时，恰恰加速了风险的累积。
封锁出口导致能量无法释放、反馈无法进入，最终将系统推向更大的风险敞口。

When the old structure uses "blockade" to respond to risk, it actually accelerates the accumulation of risk.
Blocking exits prevents energy from being released and feedback from entering, ultimately pushing the system toward an even larger risk exposure.
