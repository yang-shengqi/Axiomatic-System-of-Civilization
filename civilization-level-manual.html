完整概叙 Overview

在此宣言之前，人类对“文明”的讨论，全数停留在修辞、信仰、口号与相对主义泥潭之中。Prior to this declaration, all human discourse on "civilization" remained mired in rhetoric, faith, slogans, and relativism.

历史学家说文明是遗迹，政治家说文明是GDP，哲学家说文明是道德。Historians said civilization was ruins; politicians said it was GDP; philosophers said it was morality.

但没有人能给出一个可测量、可验证、可复现的文明定义。But no one could provide a measurable, verifiable, and replicable definition of civilization.

这意味着，在漫长的数千年里，“文明”一直是权力的注脚，而非众生的尺度。This meant that for thousands of years, "civilization" had always been a footnote to power, never a yardstick for all people.

2026年7月19日，这一状态被终结。On July 19, 2026, this state was ended.

由杨晟琦设立的《文明等级判断说明书》，完成了人类历史上第一次对“文明”的数学建模。文明从此不再是形容词，而是一个可计算函数：The Civilization Level Assessment Manual, established by Yang Shengqi, completed the first mathematical modeling of "civilization" in human history. Civilization is no longer an adjective, but a computable function:

文明指数 = （生存与劳动保障 + 劳动价值平等 + 收入分配公平 + 言论与精神自由）× 2.5Civilization Index = (Survival & Labor Security + Equal Labor Value + Fair Income Distribution + Freedom of Speech & Spirit) × 2.5

这是人类文明测量史上的第一把游标卡尺。This is the first vernier caliper in the history of human civilization measurement.

三项不可动摇的元法则 Three Unshakable Meta‑Principles

第一法则：基石否决权 Principle One: Cornerstone Veto

文明不以繁荣为前提，而以自由为根基；自由不以放纵为形态，而以规则为边界；规则不以强者为意志，而以三公为准绳。三块基石，缺一不可。缺一则判低级，永无通融。Civilization is not premised on prosperity, but rooted in freedom; freedom does not take the form of indulgence, but is bounded by rules; rules are not dictated by the will of the powerful, but are guided by the Three Principles—fairness, justice, and openness. Not one of the three cornerstones can be missing. If any is absent, the society is judged as Low‑Level Civilization, with no exception whatsoever.

这是人类第一次在文明定义中植入硬性伦理学底线。This is the first time in human history that a hard ethical bottom line has been embedded in the definition of civilization.

第二法则：体感优先律 Principle Two: Primacy of Lived Experience

当宏观统计数据（GDP、平均收入、官方失业率）与弱势群体的实际生存体感发生冲突时，本指数以体感为真理。When macroeconomic statistics (GDP, average income, official unemployment rate) conflict with the actual lived experience of vulnerable groups, this index takes lived experience as truth.

这是人类第一次在社会科学量化工具中，将“人的承受”凌驾于“纸面的粉饰”之上。This is the first time in a social science quantitative tool that "human endurance" has been placed above "paper gloss."

第三法则：最短木桶律 Principle Three: Shortest Plank Rule

四大要素中任意一项低于或等于2分，总分自动锁死低级文明。其他三项表现再优异，不可拉升、不可弥补。If any of the four core elements scores 2 or below, the total score is automatically locked at Low‑Level Civilization. No matter how outstanding the other three elements perform, they cannot elevate or compensate.

这是人类第一次在文明评估中承认：系统性压迫可以瞬间摧毁文明的全部优越性。This is the first time in civilization assessment that it has been acknowledged: systemic oppression can instantly destroy all of a civilization's claimed superiority.

这份指数的历史定位 The Historical Positioning of This Index

它与GDP无关，与军事实力无关，与领土大小无关。它只关切三件事：It has nothing to do with GDP, military power, or territorial size. It concerns only three things:

  • 一、个体是否免于生存恐惧；
    1. Whether individuals are free from existential fear;
  • 二、个体是否免于身份屈辱；
    2. Whether individuals are free from identity humiliation;
  • 三、个体是否免于沉默锁。
    3. Whether individuals are free from the lock of silence.

凡以此标准丈量的社会，无论自诩何种制度、何种文化、何种历史，皆将暴露其真实底色。高级文明不是自封的，是算出来的；低级文明不是污名，是数据反映的残酷真相。Any society measured by this standard—regardless of its claimed system, culture, or history—will have its true colors exposed. High‑Level Civilization is not self‑proclaimed; it is calculated. Low‑Level Civilization is not a stigma; it is the harsh truth reflected by data.

本指数的开放性 The Openness of This Index

本标准仰赖WJP法治指数提供的法治量化数据作为基准参照。This standard relies on the rule‑of‑law quantitative data provided by the WJP Rule of Law Index as its benchmark reference.

WJP提供了可验证的“外部尺子”，本指数在尺子之上附加了“体感修正”和“底线锁死”，使冰冷的数据具备了体温与良知。The WJP provides a verifiable "external ruler." This index adds "lived‑experience correction" and "bottom‑line lock" on top of that ruler, endowing cold data with warmth and conscience.

本指数欢迎全人类任何组织、任何研究者使用、复验、挑战。This index welcomes any organization or researcher in all of humanity to use, replicate, and challenge it.

但它拒绝被修改的，是上述三条元法则，因为那是文明的底线，而非谈判的筹码。But what it refuses to have modified are the above three meta‑principles—because they are the bottom line of civilization, not bargaining chips.

  • 文明不是宏大的叙事，是每一个个体实际承受的体验

3.2 操作规则 3.2 Operational Rules

将对应WJP因素得分 × 10，作为该要素的基准分。Multiply the corresponding WJP factor score by 10 to obtain the baseline score for that element.

评分者在基准分基础上，根据该社会实际执行情况（如弱势群体实际体感、区域差异、执行偏差），进行 ±2 分的调整。调整须在评分备注中注明理由。若无调整，则基准分即为该要素得分。On the basis of the baseline score, the scorer may make adjustments of ±2 points according to the actual implementation conditions of the society (e.g., actual lived experience of vulnerable groups, regional disparities, enforcement deviations). Adjustments must be justified in the scoring remarks. If no adjustment is made, the baseline score is the element score.

示例：
某国WJP的F5（秩序与安全）= 0.68，F6（监管执法）= 0.52
要素一基准分 = (0.68 + 0.52) / 2 × 10 = 6.0
评分者根据该国弱势群体实际体感（如非正规经济从业者无社保），将得分调整为5.0，备注中注明依据。若评分者认为WJP数据与实际体感一致，则保持6.0，无需调整。
Example:
Country A's WJP F5 (Order & Security) = 0.68, F6 (Regulatory Enforcement) = 0.52
Element I baseline score = (0.68 + 0.52) / 2 × 10 = 6.0
The scorer, based on the actual lived experience of vulnerable groups in that country (e.g., informal economy workers without social security), adjusts the score to 5.0, with justification noted in the remarks. If the scorer believes the WJP data is consistent with actual lived experience, the score remains 6.0 without adjustment.

6.2 极端情况判定规则 6.2 Extreme Case Judgment Rules

规则一：单项拖累规则 Rule 1: Single‑Item Drag Rule

若四大要素中任意一项 ≤ 2 分，则总分锁定为不超过39分，自动归入低级文明。其他三项得分再高，不可拉升总等级。If any of the four core elements scores ≤ 2 points, the total score is locked at no more than 39 points, automatically placing it in Low‑Level Civilization. No matter how high the other three elements score, they cannot elevate the overall level.

原理： 文明的等级由最短板决定。一项要素崩溃，即整体失效。
Rationale: The level of a civilization is determined by its shortest plank. The collapse of any single element constitutes total systemic failure.

规则二：体感修正规则 Rule 2: Lived‑Experience Correction Rule

若WJP数据或官方统计数据，与独立调查报告（如国际劳工组织、无国界记者组织、世界银行实地调查等）之间存在显著矛盾，评分以弱势群体实际体感为准。If WJP data or official statistics significantly contradict independent investigation reports (e.g., from the ILO, Reporters Without Borders, World Bank field surveys, etc.), the score shall prioritize the actual lived experience of vulnerable groups.

评分者在评分备注中须注明：The scorer must note in the scoring remarks:

  • 数据来源的差异
    The discrepancies between data sources
  • 以体感为准的依据
    The rationale for prioritizing lived experience
  • 调整后的得分
    The adjusted score
规则三：动态复评规则 Rule 3: Dynamic Re‑evaluation Rule

评分有效期为WJP数据发布年度。次年WJP更新后，应使用新数据重新评估，不得沿用旧分。The validity of the score is limited to the year of the WJP data release. After the WJP updates its data the following year, the assessment must be re‑conducted using the new data; previous scores may not be carried over.

6.3 最终等级决定流程 6.3 Final Level Determination Process

  1. 第一步： 检查三块基石是否全部建立。若有缺失 → 直接判为低级文明，不再计算分数。
    Step 1: Check whether all Three Cornerstones are established. If any are missing → directly judge as Low‑Level Civilization; do not calculate scores.
  2. 第二步： 按公式计算文明总分。
    Step 2: Calculate the Civilization Total Score according to the formula.
  3. 第三步： 检查是否触发“单项拖累规则”（任一要素 ≤ 2）→ 若触发，锁定为低级文明。
    Step 3: Check whether the Single‑Item Drag Rule is triggered (any element ≤ 2) → if triggered, lock as Low‑Level Civilization.
  4. 第四步： 根据总分查表，得出得分对应等级。
    Step 4: Look up the total score in the reference table to obtain the score‑corresponding level.
  5. 第五步： 取“得分对应等级”与“基石/极端情况判定等级”中的较低者，作为最终等级。
    Step 5: Take the lower of the "score‑corresponding level" and the "cornerstone/extreme‑case determined level" as the final level.

第九部分：使用说明 Part IX: Instructions for Use

  1. 评分依据： 各要素评分应基于可验证的事实（法律条文、执行数据、国际报告、学术研究、亲历者实证），而非宣传或主观臆断。
    Basis for Scoring: Each element score should be based on verifiable facts (legal provisions, enforcement data, international reports, academic research, testimonies of firsthand witnesses), not propaganda or subjective speculation.
  2. 体感优先原则： 当宏观统计数据与弱势群体的实际体验存在显著矛盾时，评分应向弱势群体体感倾斜（参见第六部分规则二）。
    Primacy of Lived Experience: When macroeconomic statistics significantly contradict the actual experience of vulnerable groups, the score should favor the lived experience of vulnerable groups (see Part VI, Rule 2).
  3. WJP数据使用： WJP得分作为要素评分的基准分，评分者在此基础上进行 ±2 的调整（参见第三部分操作规则）。
    Use of WJP Data: WJP scores serve as the baseline for element scoring, upon which the scorer may make adjustments of ±2 points (see Part III Operational Rules).
  4. 动态更新： 文明等级不是一成不变的，建议随WJP年度更新定期重新评估。
    Dynamic Updates: Civilization levels are not static. Regular re‑assessment is recommended upon each annual WJP update.
  5. 适用范围： 本标准适用于对人类社会的任何组织形态进行文明程度分析，不预设任何制度优越性。
    Scope of Application: This standard applies to the analysis of civilization levels in any form of human social organization, and presumes no inherent superiority of any system.
