The Misalignment of Justice, Interests, and Rules

正义、利益与规则的错位：系统自我维护与外部纠偏的张力分析

张力分析
ORCID: 0009-0008-3755-8960 全球首发: uncienine.wordpress.com

社会基石：文明以自由为根基，自由以规则为边界，规则以公平、公正、公开为准绳。 The Foundation of Society: Civilization is rooted in freedom; freedom is bounded by rules; rules are guided by fairness, justice, and openness.

当下情况 The Current Situation

正义成为了底层百姓最后的压舱石，利益成为了得利者的压舱石。 Justice has become the last ballast for ordinary people; interests have become the ballast for the advantaged.

最终结果 The Final Result

当两块石头碰撞时，底层百姓用直觉报警，而得利者用规则免责。 When the two ballasts collide, ordinary people sound the alarm through intuition, while the advantaged use rules to immunize themselves.

体感胜于一切庞大叙事 Lived Experience Transcends All Grand Narratives

任何体系，无论其宣称的目标多么崇高，最终的合法性都落在每一个具体个体所承受的实际体验上。 Any system — regardless of how noble its proclaimed goals — ultimately derives its legitimacy from the actual experience borne by every concrete individual.

系统的自我维护惯性 The Inertia of System Self‑Preservation

凡是权力高度集中且缺乏有效外部制衡的系统，其"自我纠偏"的驱动力必然弱于"自我维护"的惯性。 In any system where power is highly concentrated and lacks effective external checks and balances, the driving force for "self‑correction" will inevitably be weaker than the inertia of "self‑preservation."

这不是道德判断，而是系统论的基本常识。需要外力（比如危机、舆论、或内部的代际更替）来打破平衡。 This is not a moral judgment — it is basic systems theory. External forces (such as crises, public opinion, or internal generational succession) are required to break the equilibrium.

外力能否真正改变平衡？ Can External Forces Truly Shift the Equilibrium?

取决于它是否转化为制度内生的纠偏机制，而非仅仅是一次可以被消化的事件。 It depends on whether they can be transformed into institutionally endogenous correction mechanisms — rather than merely being an event that can be absorbed and digested.
