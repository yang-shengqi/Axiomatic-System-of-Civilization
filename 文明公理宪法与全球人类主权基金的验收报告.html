三大基石（《三大基石：一种文明系统的底层重构框架》）：

  • 文明基石：自由是前提，规则是边界，公平公正公开是规则的唯一合法性来源 Foundation of Civilization: Freedom is the prerequisite, rules are the boundaries, and fairness, justice, and openness are the sole source of rule legitimacy
  • 社会基石：底层百姓用直觉报警，得利者用规则免责；系统必须默认直觉为合法输入信号 Foundation of Society: Ordinary people use intuitive alarms, the advantaged use rules for immunity; the system must treat intuition as a legitimate input signal by default
  • 治理基石：判决前不可触碰人身与财产；系统性代价由系统承担；不公感知为合法输入 Foundation of Governance: No encroachment on person or property before verdict; systemic costs borne by the system; perception of injustice is a legitimate input

三条元法则（《文明等级判断说明书》）：

  • 基石否决权：三基石缺一则判低级 Cornerstone Veto: If any of the three cornerstones is missing, the society is judged Low‑Level

  • 最短木桶律：任一单项≤2分，总分自动锁死低级 Shortest Plank Rule: If any single element scores ≤2, the total score is automatically locked at Low‑Level

文化≠文明（《第八篇：评论区》）： 文化是内容，文明是框架；科技存储了文化，不等于文明进步 Culture ≠ Civilization: Culture is content; civilization is framework. Technological storage of culture does not equal civilizational progress.

全民基金（《地球的文明之书》）：

  • 资金来源：国家行政垄断行业超额利润（非征收、非国有化、非再分配，是"归还"）Funding Source: Excess profits from state‑administered monopoly industries (not expropriation, not nationalization, not redistribution — it is "restitution")
  • 法律铁索（三条永久底线）：政府不得抽离本金与收益；公民跟投权查询权赎回权受保障；境内外投资者税负绝对平等 Legal Chains (Three Permanent Bottom Lines): Government may not withdraw principal or returns; citizens' co‑investment, query, and redemption rights are protected; absolute tax equality between domestic and foreign investors
  • 三重剥离：财产权剥离至法人、人事权剥离于公职、受益权脱离政治权利 Threefold Severance: Property rights severed to a legal entity; personnel severed from public office; beneficiary rights severed from political rights
  • 三层次物理隔绝：注册香港、托管于全球独立银行、分配经区块链直达 Three‑Tier Physical Isolation: Registered in Hong Kong; assets custodied with global independent banks; distributed via blockchain
  • 核心设计：即使创始国发生政变、政府更迭、战争，基金资产在法律和物理上均不可触及 Core Design: Even if the founding state experiences a coup, regime change, or war, the fund's assets remain legally and physically untouchable
  • 善意后门：给旧系统既得利益者体面退路，避免其"抱着炸药包冲进来" Goodwill Back Door: Provides a dignified exit for vested interests of the old system, preventing them from "rushing in with explosives"

七条方法论（《改变底层逻辑的方法论》）：

  • 重定义系统的唯一目的 —— 根除不公起点 Redefine the system's sole purpose — Eliminates the origin of injustice
  • 让权力获得持续授权的机制 —— 防止锁死 Institutionalize continuous authorization of power — Prevents lock‑in
  • 责任归属逆转 —— 防止错位与矛盾 Reverse liability attribution — Prevents misalignment and contradiction
  • 裁判权与执行权永久物理隔离 —— 防止权力滥用 Permanent physical separation of adjudicative and enforcement powers — Prevents power abuse
  • 将"信任"作为系统的核心指标 —— 提前预警收缩 Make "trust" the system's core metric — Early warning against contraction
  • 释放被锁死的大多数 —— 防止逃逸与空心 Unlock the locked‑out majority — Prevents escape and hollowing
  • 制度化的"代际纠偏" —— 封堵锁死与失聪 Institutionalize intergenerational correction — Blocks lock‑in and deafness
