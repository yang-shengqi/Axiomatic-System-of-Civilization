Global Human Sovereign Fund: Reconstructing the Institutional Blueprint for the Interest Landscape Between China and the Global Community

苍穹之下万民先，山河之间国为殿：为地球生命筑底的永续契约 A perpetual covenant to build a foundation for all life on Earth

引言 Introduction

For thousands of years of human societal operation, it has been entangled in a periodic predicament: the accumulation of wealth inevitably leads to its concentration; the concentration of wealth inevitably leads to the monopolization of power; the monopolization of power inevitably solidifies into unfair distribution; unfair distribution inevitably accumulates class hatred; the outbreak of hatred inevitably destroys the existing order. After the old order collapses, a new order is established on the ruins, and then a new cycle begins. 人类社会运行数千年来，始终被一个周期性的困局所缠绕：财富的积累必然导致财富的集中，财富的集中必然引发权力的垄断，权力的垄断必然固化为分配的不公，分配的不公必然积累阶层的仇恨，仇恨的爆发必然摧毁已有的秩序。旧秩序崩塌之后，新的秩序在废墟上建立，然后开始新一轮的循环。

This cycle runs through all of recorded human history. It disregards ideology, cultural tradition, or geographical boundaries. It asks only one question: To whom does the ownership of public wealth ultimately belong? 这个循环贯穿了人类有文字记载的全部历史。它不问意识形态，不问文化传统，不问地理疆域。它只问一件事：公共财富的归属权，究竟属于谁？

So far, humanity has only two answers to this question: either it belongs to a "divinely ordained" monarch, or it belongs to an "abstract" people—and the latter, in operational terms, eventually always alienates into the former: nominal ownership by all, in reality controlled by the few. 迄今为止，人类对这个问题的回答只有两种：要么属于"神授"的君主，要么属于"抽象"的人民——而后者在操作层面最终也总是异化为前者：名义上的全民所有，实质上的少数人控制。

This plan attempts to offer a third answer: Public wealth belongs to no one; it can only be returned to everyone. And the method of return is not a one-time distribution, not an equalization of resources, not a violent revolutionary expropriation, but an irreversible institutional design—making wealth, legally, physically, and governance-wise, completely detach from any individual, family, political party, or administrative body, becoming an inherent and inalienable legal right of all citizens.

本方案试图给出第三种回答：公共财富不属于任何人，它只能被归还给所有人。而归还的方式，不是一次性的分配，不是平均主义的削平，不是暴力革命的剥夺，而是一套不可逆的制度设计——让财富在法律上、物理上、治理上，彻底脱离任何个人、家族、政党或行政机构的控制，成为全体国民与生俱来、不可剥夺的法定权益。

愿景 Vision

If one day, humanity looks back at this moment, they will find that those nightmares that have entangled us for thousands of years—war, poverty, disease—are not invincible destinies. 如果有一天，人类回望此刻，会发现那些纠缠了我们数千年的噩梦——战争、贫穷、疾病——并非不可战胜的天命。

War stems from contention. When everyone's survival no longer depends on anyone's grace, when plundering can no longer secure any extra guarantee, the logic of weapons will dry up from the root. 战争，源于争夺。当每一个人的生存不再仰赖任何人的恩赐，当抢夺无法再获得任何多余的保障，武器的逻辑便会从根部枯竭。

Poverty stems from scarcity. When public wealth is returned to its creators, when every life from birth possesses an inalienable foundation for survival, "scarcity" is revealed as a lie. 贫穷，源于匮乏。当公共财富被归还给它的创造者，当每一个生命从出生起便拥有一份不可剥夺的生存基础，"匮乏"便被揭露为一个谎言。

Disease—the most stubborn pain of biological instinct—still requires medicine to conquer. But at least, humanity will no longer lose the chance for treatment because of poverty, no longer overdraw their bodies due to survival pressure, and no longer give up hope because of despair. 疾病——那最顽固的生物本能之痛——依然需要医学去攻克。但至少，人类将不再因为贫穷而失去救治的机会，不再因为生存的压力而透支身体，不再因为绝望而放弃希望。

These three ancient enemies will not retreat amidst cheers. They will only, one day, be calmly written into history textbooks by future generations—as evidence that humanity once lived in scarcity and fear, not as a permanent destiny.

这三个古老的敌人，不会在欢呼声中退场。它们只会在某一天，被后人平静地写进历史课本——作为人类曾经活在稀缺与恐惧中的证据，而非永久的宿命。

起源语境 Origin Context

The theoretical starting point of this plan is China—an economy with a massive stock of state-owned capital, a deep tradition of administrative monopolies, and intense tension in wealth distribution. 本方案的理论起点，是中国——一个具有庞大国有资本存量、深厚行政垄断传统、以及剧烈财富分化张力的经济体。

In China's reality, the accumulation and distribution of national wealth present a profound paradox: on one hand, the state owns the world's largest operational state-owned assets, with massive net asset value, and huge annual excess profits generated by monopolistic industries such as tobacco, oil, telecommunications, and finance. On the other hand, the wealth Gini coefficient, which reflects disparities in wealth ownership, remains high over the long term, with the wealthiest 10% of the population controlling approximately 68% of the society's wealth, while the total wealth held by the bottom 50% of the population is often less than 5% of the society's total wealth. The concentration of wealth is not purely a natural result of market competition, but a concomitant phenomenon of administrative power deeply participating in resource allocation and interest distribution. 在中国的现实中，国民财富的积累与分配呈现出一种深刻的悖论：一方面，国家拥有全球最大规模的经营性国有资产，净资产规模庞大，以及烟草、石油、电信、金融等垄断行业每年产生的巨额超额利润。另一方面，反映财富占有差距的财富基尼系数长期处于高位，最富有的前10%人群掌握了约68%的社会财富，而底层50%的人口所拥有的财富总和往往不足全社会总财富的5%。财富的集中，并非纯粹市场竞争的自然结果，而是行政权力深度参与资源配置和利益分配的伴生现象。

This is not a problem unique to China. Any country with large-scale administrative monopolies and state-owned capital faces the same structural dilemma: when the ownership of public wealth heavily overlaps with private power, "public ownership" effectively alienates into "official ownership" at the operational level, while the ultimate rights of all citizens as owners have always lacked an institutionalized channel for realization. 这并非中国独有的问题。世界上任何存在大规模行政垄断和国有资本的国家，都面临同样的结构性困境：当公共财富的归属权与私人权力高度重叠时，"公有制"便在操作层面异化为"官有制"，而全体国民作为终极所有者的权益，始终缺少一个制度化的实现通道。

China thus becomes the earliest laboratory of ideas for this plan. Many of the plan's key technical constraints—such as how to prevent a strong administrative system from re-establishing de facto control after the fund's establishment, and how to ensure that the right to benefit is not deprived due to a citizen's political stance—originate from targeted responses to China's governance characteristics. However, once completed, these designs prove equally applicable to any country with similar power structures. 中国因此成为本方案最早的思想实验室。方案中的许多关键技术约束——比如如何防止一个强势的行政体系在基金成立后重新建立事实控制，如何确保受益权不因国民的政治态度而被剥夺——都源自对中国治理特征的针对性回应。但这些设计一旦完成，便发现它们同样适用于任何存在类似权力结构的国家。

This plan starts from this local cross-section but ultimately points to a common human proposition that transcends any national border. 本方案从这个本土剖面出发，却最终指向了超越任何一个国家边界的人类共同命题。

问题的起点：六重断裂 The Starting Point of the Problem: Sixfold Ruptures

断裂一：所有制与获得感之间的断裂 Rupture One: The Rupture Between Ownership and Sense of Gain

China possesses the world's largest monopolistic state-owned assets—China Tobacco's annual tax and profit exceeds 1.6 trillion RMB, and monopolistic profits from industries like oil, electricity, and telecommunications are equally enormous. These wealth sources fundamentally originate from resources owned by all the people and market monopoly privileges, and should therefore belong to all citizens. 中国拥有全球规模最大的垄断性国有资产——中国烟草年税利超1.6万亿元，石油、电力、电信等行业的垄断利润同样巨大。这些财富本质上来源于全民所有的资源和市场垄断特权，理应属于全体国民。

However, under the current system, most of these enormous profits flow directly into the national treasury for use in annual budget expenditures. Ordinary citizens have little intuitive perception of this, and the concept of "state-owned assets belonging to all the people" remains largely conceptual, failing to translate into visible, tangible, and disposable property income in the name of each individual. 然而，在现行体制下，这些巨额利润大多直接进入国家财政，用于年度预算支出。普通民众对此缺乏直观感受，"国有资产全民所有"更多停留在概念层面，未能转化为每个人名下可见、可感、可支配的财产性收入。

断裂二：体制内与体制外的断裂 Rupture Two: The Rupture Between Inside and Outside the System

Civil servants have annuities, stable expectations, and systemic protection. Private sector employees, gig workers, and the unemployed do not. Society faces the anxiety of "getting old before getting rich" and the fairness anxiety caused by the welfare gap between those inside and outside the system. This is not a Gini coefficient problem. It is a deep-seated tear of "you and I are not in the same system." 公务员有年金、有稳定预期、有体制庇护。私企员工、灵活就业者、失业人员没有。社会面临"未富先老"的养老焦虑、体制内外福利鸿沟引发的公平焦虑。这不是基尼系数问题。这是"你和我不在一个系统里"的深层撕裂。

断裂三：中国与世界的信任断裂 Rupture Three: The Rupture of Trust Between China and the World

"Decoupling" has become the global narrative. The outside world does not believe that Chinese capital can flow in and out freely without arbitrary intervention. China does not fully trust external rules. Both sides spin their wheels in suspicion. "脱钩"成为全球叙事。外部世界不相信中国的资本可以自由进出、不受任意干预。中国也不完全信任外部规则。双方在猜疑中空转。

断裂四：时间维度的断裂——决策短视 Rupture Four: The Rupture of the Time Dimension — Short-Termism in Decision-Making

Any leadership under any system has a "short-term performance bias"—they want to see results within their term. Five or ten years is already considered long-term. Thirty years? That's for the next few terms. This is not a moral issue; it is an issue of incentive structure. 任何体制下的领导层，都有"短期政绩偏好"——我要在我的任期内看到成果。五年、十年已经算长期，三十年？那是下几任的事。这不是道德问题，是激励结构问题。

断裂五：代际断裂——年轻人不买了 Rupture Five: The Generational Rupture — The Younger Generation Isn't Buying In

Young people see the "pension gap," "delayed retirement," and the "35-year-old crisis." After rational calculation, they conclude: "I won't live to see the day I get the money, or the money I get won't be worth anything." Someone working a gig job pays over 1,000 RMB a month in contributions, taking up 1/3 to 1/2 of their income. Young people are turning to more extreme forms of self-rescue—"FIRE" (Financial Independence, Retire Early), "save up 2 million for interest," or simply "lying flat." 年轻人看到"养老金缺口""延迟退休""35岁危机"，理性计算后得出一个结论——"我活不到领钱那天，或者领的钱根本不值钱。"灵活就业一个月交一千多，占收入的1/3甚至1/2。年轻人转向更极端的自救——"FIRE"（财务独立、早早退休）、"存够200万吃利息"，或者干脆"躺平"。

断裂六：人性短视的普遍性 Rupture Six: The Universality of Human Short-Sightedness

This is not China's problem. It is all of humanity's problem. All people—fund managers, officials, ordinary citizens—have a "present bias": current benefits outweigh future benefits. This is code written into our brains by evolution. 这不是中国的问题。这是全人类的问题。所有人——基金经理、官员、普通百姓——都有"现时偏好"：眼前的利益比未来的利益更重。这是进化写进大脑的代码。

Three institutional ruptures + two time-dimensional ruptures + one fundamental human rupture = the complete set of problems that the institutional design must address.

三重断裂 + 两个时间维度断裂 + 一个人性底层断裂 = 制度设计必须面对的完整问题集。

第一章：总纲 Chapter One: General Principles

1.1 核心洞见：财富本不稀缺 1.1 Core Insight: Wealth is Not Inherently Scarce

The underlying assumption of all conflicts in human society is that "survival resources are naturally scarce, hence they must be contested." But this assumption itself needs to be examined. 人类社会一切冲突的底层假设，是"生存资源天然稀缺，所以必须争夺"。但这个假设本身需要被审视。

The real scarcity is not a lack of total volume, but the monopolization of the ownership of this public wealth by a very few individuals at the very inception of the institutional design.

真正的匮乏不是总量不足，而是这些公共财富的归属权，在制度设计之初就被极少数人所垄断。

The core insight of this plan is: if this portion of public wealth, inherently belonging to all citizens, is returned to everyone at its source through an irreversible institutional design, the illusion of scarcity will dissipate at the root. When everyone's basic survival no longer depends on dependence on a particular employer, a particular institution, a particular benefactor, the most fundamental "scrambling" motive in human society will be systematically weakened. 本方案的核心洞见是：如果在源头将这部分本应属于全体国民的公共财富，通过不可逆的制度设计归还给所有人，稀缺的幻象就会从根上消解。当每个人的基本生存不再依赖于对某个雇主、某个机构、某个恩主的依附，人类社会最底层的"争抢"动机就会被系统性削弱。

This is not a plan about "how to make society fairer." It is a plan about "how to enable civilization to break out of its death loop." 这不是一个关于"如何让社会更公平"的方案，这是一个关于"如何让文明跳出死循环"的方案。

1.2 方案定义 1.2 Definition of the Plan

The Human Wealth Fund, in its ultimate form, will be the Earth Human Basic Survival Bottom-Line Trust. 人类财富基金，其最终形态为地球人类基本生存底线信托。

It does not belong to any country, any institution, or any particular group. It belongs only to every specific, irreplaceable life form with equal beneficiary rights. When a newborn baby, whether born in a war-torn land or a bustling metropolis, possesses a completely equal and inalienable account, only then does this institution truly transcend countries, nations, and ideologies, achieving the most thorough return to the concept of "humanity." 它不属于任何国家、任何机构、任何特定群体。它只属于每一个具体的、不可替代的、享有平等受益权的生命体。当一个刚出生的婴儿，无论他出生在战乱之地还是繁华都市，都拥有一个完全平等、不可剥夺的账户时，这个制度才真正超越了国家、民族、意识形态，实现了对"人类"这个概念最彻底的回归。

In operational terms, it is a permanent institutional arrangement with the excess profits of national administrative monopolistic industries and public accumulation as the initial capital source, independent legislation as legal protection, complete divestiture and physical isolation as governance principles, perpetual lock-in of initial capital as a safety bottom line, global multi-asset allocation as a means of value appreciation, an open tiered account system as the beneficiary structure, and with all beneficiary accounts receiving returns in proportion to their contributed capital. 在操作层面，它是一个以各国行政垄断行业超额利润及公共积累为初始资本来源、以单独立法为法律保障、以完整剥离和物理隔绝为治理原则、以初始资金永恒锁定为安全底线、以全球多元资产配置为增值手段、以开放分级账户体系为受益结构、全体受益账户均按出资比例获取收益的永久性制度安排。

Its governance rights are exercised jointly by the citizens and institutions of all participating countries, in proportion to their contributed capital. No single country, institution, or individual may monopolize or dominate the governance rights of the fund. The sole purpose of the fund is to provide an inalienable basic survival bottom-line guarantee for every life form on Earth. 其治理权按注入资金比例，由全球所有参与国国民及机构共同行使。任何单一国家、机构或个人，均不得独占或垄断基金的治理权。基金的唯一目的，是为地球上每一个生命体提供不可剥夺的基本生存底线保障。

第二章：制度引擎——创始资金的来源与性质 Chapter Two: Institutional Engine — Source and Nature of Founding Capital

2.1 为什么必须是垄断行业利润？ 2.1 Why Must It Be Monopolistic Industry Profits?

The starting point of any institutional design is "where does the money come from?" If this question is not answered well, the entire institutional edifice will rot from its foundation. 任何制度设计的起点，是"钱从哪里来"。这个问题回答不好，整个制度的大厦就会从地基开始腐烂。

Monopolistic profits do not belong to the institutions that operate them.

垄断利润不属于经营它的机构。

The decisive factor enabling an industry to generate excess profits far exceeding the level of market competition is not its operational capability, but the exclusive power granted to it by the state in the name of all citizens. These powers include: restrictions on market entry, exclusivity in resource occupation, protection of pricing power, and legal barriers to competition. 一个行业之所以能产生远超市场竞争水平的超额利润，决定性因素不是它的经营能力，而是国家以全体国民的名义，赋予它的排他性权力。这些权力包括：市场准入的限制、资源占有的排他性、定价权的保护、竞争的法律屏障。

All citizens have tacitly consented to the restriction of their consumption choices, tacitly consented to the exclusive occupation of public resources by specific institutions, and tacitly consented to the distortion of market competition by administrative forces—this series of "tacit consents" constitutes the true source of monopolistic profits. 全体国民默许了自己的消费选择权被限制，默许了公共资源被特定机构独占，默许了市场竞争被行政力量扭曲——这一系列"默许"，构成了垄断利润的真正来源。

Injecting monopolistic profits into a universal fund is not taxation, not nationalization, not wealth redistribution. It is restitution—the restitution of a public wealth that has been held in proxy for a long time but has never truly been returned. 将垄断利润注入全民基金，不是征收，不是国有化，不是财富再分配。它是归还——归还一笔被长期代持却从未真正交还的公共财富。

2.2 这一资金来源的国际法律与道德审查 2.2 International Legal and Moral Review of This Funding Source

Under the international legal framework, the capital injection behavior of this plan does not constitute any defined "expropriation" or "nationalization." The reasons are: 在国际法框架下，本方案的注资行为不构成任何定义的"征收"或"国有化"。原因是：

  • First, the right to operate a monopolistic industry and the ownership of monopolistic profits are, in international investment law practice, always two separate legal objects. The disposal right of a state over monopolistic industry profits falls within the policy space of sovereign actors and has never been fully excluded by bilateral investment treaties or international investment arbitration. 第一，垄断行业的经营权与垄断利润的所有权，在国际投资法实践中始终是分离的两个法律客体。国家对垄断行业利润的处置权，属于主权行为体的政策空间，从未被双边投资协定或国际投资仲裁完全排除。
  • Second, even if it enters arbitration, the promoting country can clearly argue: this is not depriving investors of their legitimate rights, but making a long-overdue ownership adjustment to a source of income that is naturally public in nature. 第二，即便进入仲裁程序，推动国也可以清晰论证：这不是剥夺投资者的合法权益，而是对一种天然具有公共属性的收入来源，进行迟到的归属调整。
  • Third, at the level of global ethical consensus, "monopolistic excess profits returning to society" is a cross-ideological consensus. 第三，在全球伦理共识层面，"垄断暴利回归社会"是跨意识形态的普遍共识。

2.3 规模效应：大到无法忽视 2.3 Scale Effect: Too Big to Be Ignored

Another key reason why this plan selects monopolistic industry profits as its founding capital is scale. 本方案选择垄断行业利润作为创始资金的另一个关键原因，是规模。

A "small sum" of universal dividends is politically welfare, economically supplementary, and psychologically a gift. It cannot change the social contract, cannot build a community of shared interests, and cannot stop the inertia of contention. 一笔"小钱"的全民分红，在政治上是福利，在经济上是补充，在心理上是恩赐。它无法改变社会契约，无法建立利益共同体，无法阻止争夺的惯性。

But the scale of monopolistic industry profits naturally solves this problem. Globally, the annual total profit of administrative monopolistic industries in any country accounts for a non-negligible proportion of its national economy. Taking China as an example, the annual total profit of major monopolistic industries such as tobacco, oil, electricity, and telecommunications accounted for approximately 5%-8% of GDP. If a considerable proportion of these profits is completely divested in one go and injected into the fund, the resulting initial capital pool will be a figure that no political force can ignore. 但垄断行业利润的规模，天然地解决了这个问题。在全球范围内，任何国家的行政垄断行业的年利润总额，在其所在国经济中均占有不可忽视的比重。以中国为例，烟草、石油、电力、通信等主要垄断行业年利润总额约占GDP的5%-8%。将这些利润的相当比例一次性完整剥离并注入基金，所产生的初始资本池，将是一个任何政治力量都无法忽视的数字。

This scale means: for citizens, the income it generates is sufficient to change a family's survival baseline; for international capital markets, a sovereign fund with massive monopolistic profits as its starting principal and legally completely independent will, from its inception, be a target for the world's top financial institutions; for any potential tamperer, when the fund's scale reaches a level where all citizens can see and touch it, any attempt to touch it is an act of enmity against all citizens. 3.1 单独立法 3.1 Independent Legislation

Meaning

The fund must be established with the status of constitutional law. So-called "constitutional law" means: 基金必须以宪法性法律的地位设立。所谓"宪法性法律"，意味着：

  • Its legal rank is higher than all ordinary laws, administrative regulations, local regulations, and departmental rules. 其法律位阶高于所有普通法律、行政法规、地方性法规和部门规章。
  • Any legal provisions conflicting with the fund's basic law are automatically invalid. 任何与基金基本法相抵触的法律条款均自动无效。
  • Amendments to the fund's basic law must pass an extremely high threshold far exceeding that of ordinary legislation. 基金基本法的修改，必须经过远超普通立法的极高门槛。
  • No head of the executive branch has the authority to modify, freeze, or repeal any provision of the fund's basic law by executive order. 任何行政首脑，均无权以行政命令的方式修改、冻结或废除基金基本法的任何条款。

  • The independence of the fund's governance structure and the selection mechanism 基金治理结构的独立性和遴选机制
  • Conditions for the fund's dissolution—must pass by an absolute majority in a popular referendum 基金解散的条件——必须在全民公决中以绝对多数票通过

1. Legislation prohibits any government organ from withdrawing the fund's principal and returns in any form.

From the date of its establishment, the National Wealth Sovereign Fund is legally and completely separated from government finances. The fund's tax exemption treatment applies without discrimination to Chinese mainland residents, Hong Kong residents, and all qualified overseas investors. Tax exemption is the natural attribute of this asset. 其三，立法保障境内外投资者税负绝对平等。该基金的免税待遇，无差别适用于中国内地居民、香港居民及全球所有合规境外投资者。免税是这笔资产的天然属性。

3.3 完整剥离 3.3 Complete Divestiture

First Layer: Complete Divestiture of Property Rights——The ownership of assets allocated to the fund is legally and completely transferred to the fund's legal entity. Upon completion of the transfer, the original owner retains no right of recourse, beneficiary rights, mortgage rights, or pledge rights in law. 第一重：财产权的完整剥离——划入基金的资产，其所有权在法律上完全转移至基金法人名下。转移完成后，原所有者不再保留任何追索权、受益权、抵押权、质押权。

Second Layer: Complete Divestiture of Personnel Rights——No member of the fund's highest decision-making body may be appointed or nominated by incumbent or former public officials of the founding country, senior officials of any political party in the founding country, or any person with direct interest links to monopolistic industries. 第二重：人事权的完整剥离——基金的最高决策机构的任何成员，不得由在职或离任的创始国公职人员、政党高级官员、垄断行业利益关联人员担任或提名。

No beneficiary may be deprived of their beneficiary rights due to political stance, religious belief, or criminal conviction. 第三重：受益权的完整剥离并固化——受益人的范围和按出资比例获取收益的规则，必须写入基金基本法的正文。任何受益人不得因政治立场、宗教信仰、刑事犯罪而被剥夺其受益权。

Layer 1: Physical isolation of capital from the parent——The fund's legal place of registration must be an international financial center with a highly mature rule of law. All fund assets are custodied with globally top-tier independent custodian banks. The founding state's government, judiciary, or any individual has no freeze, query, or transfer rights over assets in custodian accounts. 第一层：资本与母体的物理隔绝——基金的法定注册地必须选择法治高度成熟的国际金融中心。基金的全部资产交由全球顶级的独立托管银行分散托管。创始国政府、司法机构或任何个人，对托管账户内的资产不具有冻结权、查询权和划拨权。

Layer 2: Physical isolation of governance from power——The fund is not subject to the directives of any institution or individual of the founding state. The fund's investment decisions, rebalancing plans, holdings details, and other internal information need not be reported to the founding state's government or head of state. Layer 3: Physical isolation of distribution from identity——The fund's beneficiary distributions do not pass through any administrative intermediaries. Each citizen's digital identity is directly bound to the fund's distribution system through blockchain or equivalent security technologies. Dividends are automatically credited without any government department's approval, allocation, or disbursement. 第三层：分配与身份的物理隔绝——基金的受益权分配不经过任何行政中间环节。每个国民的数字身份通过区块链或同等安全级别的技术手段，直接与基金的分配系统绑定。分红自动到账，不经由任何政府部门审批、拨付或代发。

Independent Legislation: Makes attacking the fund's legitimacy extremely costly—requires constitutional-amendment-level force

Physical Isolation: Renders physical means of attacking the fund ineffective—assets abroad, law abroad, governance abroad

Three locks simultaneously securing the same door. If any single lock is breached, two others remain. Every citizen, from the day of birth, possesses an individual account in the fund. From the day the account is established, dividends corresponding to the proportion of capital are automatically distributed to the beneficiary's digital account each year. Dividends are not subject to any age restrictions—received from birth, enjoyed for life. 每一位国民自出生之日起，即在基金中拥有一个人级账户。该账户自设立之日起，每年按其所对应资本比例获取的分红，自动发放至受益人数字账户。分红不受任何年龄限制，出生即领，终身享有。

The principal corresponding to the account is locked before the beneficiary reaches the age of 60; no one may withdraw, pledge, mortgage, or transfer it for any reason. After reaching the age of 60, the beneficiary may independently decide the disposition of the principal. 账户所对应的本金，在受益人年满六十周岁前处于锁定状态，任何人不得以任何理由提取、抵押、质押或转出。受益人年满六十周岁后，可自主决定本金的处置方式。

4.3.4 十年递延领取规则 4.3.4 Ten‑Year Deferred Withdrawal Rule

  • Immediate receipt portion: 70% of annual dividends, distributed directly to beneficiaries' digital accounts quarterly or monthly. 即时领取部分：年度分红的70%，按季度或月度直接发放至受益人数字账户。
  • Deferred portion: 30% of annual dividends enters the beneficiary's "deferred account," withdrawable after 10 years. 递延领取部分：年度分红的30%，进入受益人的"递延账户"，受益人需在满10年后方可提取。
  • The funds and investment returns in the deferred account are fully owned by the beneficiary. 递延账户的资金及投资收益，所有权完全归属于受益人。
  • Exceptions for early withdrawal: Beneficiaries may apply for early withdrawal upon reaching age 60 or being diagnosed with a terminal illness. 提前支取的例外：受益人年满60周岁或被确诊为绝症时可申请提前支取。

4.3.5 代际继承的年龄重置规则 4.3.5 Age Reset Rule for Generational Inheritance

The second beneficiary or testamentary heir of an individual account inherits the annual dividend beneficiary rights, not the ownership of the account's principal. Before the heir reaches the required age, the account's annual dividends are either defaulted into the fund's public income pool or rolled into the account's principal as specified by the original beneficiary. 个人级账户的第二受益人或遗嘱继承人，继承的是该账户年度分红受益权，而非账户本金的所有权。继承人未达年龄前，该账户年度分红默认归入基金公共收益池，或按原受益人指定滚存入该账户本金。

4.3.6 按出资比例分配原则 4.3.6 Distribution in Proportion to Contribution

The income distribution of all account levels is uniformly executed in proportion to each account's contributed capital in the total capital. The rule is written into the fund's charter, automatically calculated and disbursed by algorithms, with no discretionary power for anyone. 所有账户层级的收益分配，统一按各自注入资本在总资本中的比例执行。规则写入基金章程，由算法自动计算、自动发放，任何人无自由裁量权。

4.3.7 合规审查原则 4.3.7 Compliance Review Principles

All new account capital sources must pass the fund's independent compliance committee's anti-money laundering and counter-terrorism financing review. The fund does not accept capital from sources that cannot be traced or violate international anti-money laundering standards. 所有新设账户的入资来源，须经基金独立合规委员会的反洗钱与反恐怖主义融资审查。基金不接受来源无法追溯或违反国际反洗钱标准的入资。

4.3.9 禁止受益权转让 4.3.9 Prohibition on Beneficiary Rights Transfer

The beneficiary rights of any account at any level may not be transferred, pledged, mortgaged, or traded in secondary markets in any form. The fund does not recognize, execute, or register any form of beneficiary rights transfer. 任何层级的受益账户，其受益权均不得以任何形式在二级市场转让、抵押、质押或交易。基金不承认、不执行、不登记任何形式的受益权转让。

4.4 投资原则 4.4 Investment Principles

4.4.1 操盘手的限制 4.4.1 Restrictions on Fund Managers

  • Compensation deferral mechanism: Over 50% of fund managers' annual bonuses must be deferred in the form of fund shares, with a deferral period of no less than 5 years. 薪酬递延机制：操盘手年度奖金的50%以上必须以基金份额的形式递延支付，递延期限不低于5年。
  • Personal co-investment obligation: Fund managers must inject 10%-20% of their personal investable assets into the fund under the same conditions. 个人跟投义务：操盘手必须将其个人可投资资产的10%-20%以相同条件注入基金。
  • No hedging: Fund managers and their immediate family may not engage in any form of hedging transactions against the fund's positions. 禁止对冲：操盘手及其直系亲属不得对基金的持仓进行任何形式的对冲交易。
  • Post-employment quiet period: Fund managers may not join any asset management institution in direct competition with the fund within 2 years of departure. 离职静默期：操盘手离职后2年内，不得加入任何与基金有直接竞争关系的资产管理机构。
  • Lifetime accountability clause: If a fund manager is found to have committed fraud, insider trading, or illicit transfer of benefits during their tenure, the fund has the right to pursue all compensation and investment returns for life. 终身追责条款：若操盘手在任职期间或离职后被发现在基金任职期间存在欺诈、内幕交易、利益输送等行为，基金有权在其终身范围内追索其全部薪酬及投资收益。

4.4.8 一篮子货币权重的科学锚定 4.4.8 Scientific Anchoring of Basket Currency Weights

The currency exposure of the fund's total assets must be allocated according to a predetermined, publicly transparent basket currency weight, anchored to the following globally recognized authoritative reference sources:

  • Second Anchor: IMF SDR Currency Basket 第二锚：IMF的SDR（特别提款权）货币篮子
