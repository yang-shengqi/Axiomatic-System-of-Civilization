引言 Introduction

人类社会运行数千年来，始终被一个周期性的困局所缠绕：财富的积累必然导致财富的集中，财富的集中必然引发权力的垄断，权力的垄断必然固化为分配的不公，分配的不公必然积累阶层的仇恨，仇恨的爆发必然摧毁已有的秩序。旧秩序崩塌之后，新的秩序在废墟上建立，然后开始新一轮的循环。

For thousands of years, human society has been trapped in a cyclical predicament: the accumulation of wealth inevitably leads to its concentration; the concentration of wealth inevitably leads to the monopolization of power; the monopolization of power inevitably solidifies into distributive injustice; distributive injustice inevitably accumulates class resentment; the eruption of resentment inevitably destroys the existing order. After the old order collapses, a new order is built upon the ruins, and the cycle begins anew.

这个循环贯穿了人类有文字记载的全部历史。它不问意识形态，不问文化传统，不问地理疆域。它只问一件事：公共财富的归属权，究竟属于谁？

This cycle runs through all of recorded human history. It asks no questions about ideology, cultural tradition, or geographical boundaries. It asks only one thing: to whom does the ownership of public wealth belong?

迄今为止，人类对这个问题的回答只有两种：要么属于"神授"的君主，要么属于"抽象"的人民，而后者在操作层面最终也总是异化为前者：名义上的全民所有，实质上的少数人控制。

To date, humanity has had only two answers to this question: either it belongs to the "divinely ordained" monarch, or it belongs to the "abstract" people—yet the latter, at the operational level, always degenerates into the former: nominal ownership by all, effective control by a few.

本方案试图给出第三种回答：公共财富不属于任何人，它只能被归还给所有人。而归还的方式，不是一次性的分配，不是平均主义的削平，不是暴力革命的剥夺，而是一套不可逆的制度设计，让财富在法律上、物理上、治理上，彻底脱离任何个人、家族、政党或行政机构的控制，成为全体国民与生俱来、不可剥夺的法定权益。

This proposal attempts to offer a third answer: public wealth belongs to no one; it can only be returned to everyone. And the means of return is not a one‑time distribution, not leveling by egalitarianism, not expropriation by violent revolution—but an irreversible institutional design that severs wealth, legally, physically, and governancely, from the control of any individual, family, political party, or administrative body, making it an inalienable legal right of all citizens from birth.

本方案最终要回答的，不是经济问题，不是政治问题，而是人类作为个体在国家共同体中生存的四个根本需求：

This proposal ultimately seeks to answer not economic questions, not political questions, but four fundamental needs of human beings as individuals surviving within national communities:

  • 安全感，生存底线是否被制度保障，而非依赖于任何人的恩赐或市场的波动。
  • Security—whether the survival floor is institutionally guaranteed, rather than dependent on anyone's benevolence or market fluctuations.
  • 获得感，劳动和创造的成果，是否真正属于自己，而非被无形的分配机制所剥夺。
  • Gain—whether the fruits of labor and creation truly belong to oneself, rather than being expropriated by invisible distribution mechanisms.
  • 幸福感，在生存和安全之上，是否还有余裕去追求值得追求的生活。
  • Happiness—whether, beyond survival and security, there remains the margin to pursue a life worth living.
  • 参与感，是否作为共同体的正式成员而被承认，而非被排斥在公共财富的受益圈之外。
  • Participation—whether one is recognized as a formal member of the community, rather than excluded from the circle of public wealth beneficiaries.

这四个需求，在任何时代、任何制度下，都是人类对公共秩序最朴素的期待。本方案的全部设计，就是为这四个期待提供一个不可逆的制度应答。

These four needs, in any era and under any system, are humanity's most basic expectations of public order. The entire design of this proposal is to provide an irreversible institutional response to these four expectations.

本国的穷人，地球的穷人，他们不是这个方案的修辞对象，不是慈善的客体，不是选票的筹码。他们就是这个方案的主人。这个方案的每一个字，最终都是为他们写的。

The poor of this nation, the poor of the earth—they are not rhetorical objects of this proposal, not subjects of charity, not bargaining chips of votes. They are the masters of this proposal. Every word of this proposal is ultimately written for them.

如果当权者看不到他们，这个方案就不是在为任何人说话，它只是在为权力设计一件更合身的外衣。

If those in power cannot see them, this proposal is not speaking for anyone—it is merely designing a better-fitting coat for power itself.

这个方案的野心，就是让地球上最后一个村庄里最贫穷的那个孩子，从他出生的那一刻起，就和这个星球上最富有的人一样，拥有一份谁也不可夺走的生存保障。这不是因为他值得同情，而是因为他值得被当作一个完整的人。

The ambition of this proposal is to ensure that the poorest child in the last village on Earth, from the moment of birth, possesses—just like the richest person on the planet—a survival guarantee that no one can take away. Not because he deserves pity, but because he deserves to be treated as a whole person.

愿景 Vision

如果有一天，人类回望此刻，会发现那些纠缠了我们数千年的噩梦，战争、贫穷、疾病，并非不可战胜的天命。

If one day, humanity looks back upon this moment, it will find that the nightmares that have plagued us for millennia—war, poverty, disease—were not invincible destinies.

战争，源于争夺。当每一个人的生存不再仰赖任何人的恩赐，当抢夺无法再获得任何多余的保障，武器的逻辑便会从根部枯竭。

War arises from competition. When every person's survival no longer depends on anyone's benevolence, when plunder can no longer secure any surplus protection, the logic of weapons will wither at its root.

贫穷，源于匮乏。当公共财富被归还给它的创造者，当每一个生命从出生起便拥有一份不可剥夺的生存基础，"匮乏"便被揭露为一个谎言。

Poverty arises from scarcity. When public wealth is returned to its creators, when every life from birth possesses an inalienable foundation of survival, "scarcity" is exposed as a lie.

疾病，那最顽固的生物本能之痛，依然需要医学去攻克。但至少，人类将不再因为贫穷而失去救治的机会，不再因为生存的压力而透支身体，不再因为绝望而放弃希望。

Disease—that most stubborn biological pain—will still need medicine to conquer. But at least, humanity will no longer lose the chance of treatment due to poverty, no longer overdraw their bodies due to the pressure of survival, no longer abandon hope due to despair.

这三个古老的敌人，不会在欢呼声中退场。它们只会在某一天，被后人平静地写进历史课本，作为人类曾经活在稀缺与恐惧中的证据，而非永久的宿命。

These three ancient enemies will not depart amid cheers. They will only, one day, be calmly written into history textbooks by future generations—as evidence that humanity once lived in scarcity and fear, not as permanent destiny.

起源语境：一个本土的剖面 Origin Context: A Local Cross‑Section

本方案的理论起点，是中国，一个具有庞大国有资本存量、深厚行政垄断传统、以及剧烈财富分化张力的经济体。

The theoretical starting point of this proposal is China—an economy with a vast stock of state‑owned capital, a deep tradition of administrative monopoly, and intense wealth‑differentiation tensions.

在中国的现实中，国民财富的积累与分配呈现出一种深刻的悖论：一方面，国家拥有全球最大规模的经营性国有资产，净资产规模庞大，以及烟草、石油、电信、金融等垄断行业每年产生的巨额超额利润。另一方面，反映财富占有差距的财富基尼系数长期处于高位，最富有的前10%人群掌握了约68%的社会财富，而底层50%的人口所拥有的财富总和往往不足全社会总财富的5%。财富的集中，并非纯粹市场竞争的自然结果，而是行政权力深度参与资源配置和利益分配的伴生现象。

In China's reality, the accumulation and distribution of national wealth presents a profound paradox: on one hand, the state possesses the world's largest stock of operating state‑owned assets, with enormous net asset value, and the colossal excess profits generated annually by monopoly industries such as tobacco, oil, telecommunications, and finance. On the other hand, the wealth Gini coefficient—reflecting inequality in wealth distribution—has long remained elevated, with the wealthiest 10% of the population holding approximately 68% of social wealth, while the bottom 50% often possess less than 5% of total social wealth. The concentration of wealth is not a natural outcome of market competition, but a concomitant phenomenon of administrative power's deep involvement in resource allocation and interest distribution.

这并非中国独有的问题。世界上任何存在大规模行政垄断和国有资本的国家，都面临同样的结构性困境：当公共财富的归属权与私人权力高度重叠时，"公有制"便在操作层面异化为"官有制"，而全体国民作为终极所有者的权益，始终缺少一个制度化的实现通道。

This is not a problem unique to China. Any country with large‑scale administrative monopoly and state capital faces the same structural dilemma: when the ownership of public wealth heavily overlaps with private power, "public ownership" degenerates at the operational level into "official ownership," and the rights of all citizens as ultimate owners always lack an institutionalized channel of realization.

中国因此成为本方案最早的思想实验室。方案中的许多关键技术约束，比如如何防止一个强势的行政体系在基金成立后重新建立事实控制，如何确保受益权不因国民的政治态度而被剥夺，都源自对中国治理特征的针对性回应。但这些设计一旦完成，便发现它们同样适用于任何存在类似权力结构的国家。

China thus becomes the earliest thought laboratory for this proposal. Many of the proposal's key technical constraints—such as how to prevent a strong administrative system from re‑establishing de facto control after the fund's establishment, how to ensure that beneficiary rights are not revoked due to citizens' political attitudes—originate from targeted responses to China's governance characteristics. But once these designs are completed, it becomes clear that they apply equally to any country with a similar power structure.

本方案从这个本土剖面出发，却最终指向了超越任何一个国家边界的人类共同命题。

This proposal starts from this local cross‑section, yet ultimately points to a common human proposition that transcends the borders of any single nation.

问题的起点：六重断裂 The Starting Point: Six Fractures

01

断裂一：所有制与获得感之间的断裂

中国拥有全球规模最大的垄断性国有资产，中国烟草年税利超1.6万亿元，石油、电力、电信等行业的垄断利润同样巨大。这些财富本质上来源于全民所有的资源和市场垄断特权，理应属于全体国民。然而，在现行体制下，这些巨额利润大多直接进入国家财政，用于年度预算支出。普通民众对此缺乏直观感受，"国有资产全民所有"更多停留在概念层面，未能转化为每个人名下可见、可感、可支配的财产性收入。

China possesses the world's largest stock of monopolistic state‑owned assets. These wealth fundamentally derives from resources owned by all citizens. However, under the current system, most of these enormous profits go directly into state finances, failing to translate into visible, tangible property income.

02

断裂二：体制内与体制外的断裂

公务员有年金、有稳定预期、有体制庇护。私企员工、灵活就业者、失业人员没有。社会面临"未富先老"的养老焦虑、体制内外福利鸿沟引发的公平焦虑。这不是基尼系数问题。这是"你和我不在一个系统里"的深层撕裂。

Civil servants have pensions, stable expectations, and institutional protection. Private‑sector employees, flexible workers, and the unemployed do not. This is not a Gini coefficient issue. This is the deep rupture of "you and I are not in the same system."

03

断裂三：中国与世界的信任断裂

"脱钩"成为全球叙事。外部世界不相信中国的资本可以自由进出、不受任意干预。中国也不完全信任外部规则。双方在猜疑中空转。

"Decoupling" has become a global narrative. The outside world does not believe that Chinese capital can enter and exit freely. Both sides spin in mutual suspicion.

2.2 这一资金来源的国际法律与道德审查 2.2 International Legal and Moral Review of This Funding Source

在国际法框架下，本方案的注资行为不构成任何定义的"征收"或"国有化"。原因是：

Under international law, the capital injection of this proposal does not constitute "expropriation" or "nationalization" under any definition. The reasons are:

The state's power to dispose of monopoly industry profits falls within the policy space of sovereign actors and has never been entirely excluded by bilateral investment treaties or international investment arbitration.

3.1 单独立法 3.1 Independent Legislation

含义

Meaning

基金必须以宪法性法律的地位设立。所谓"宪法性法律"，意味着：

The fund must be established with the status of constitutional law. "Constitutional law" means:

  • 其法律位阶高于所有普通法律、行政法规、地方性法规和部门规章
  • Its legal rank is above all ordinary laws, administrative regulations, local regulations, and departmental rules
  • 任何与基金基本法相抵触的法律条款均自动无效
  • Any legal provisions that conflict with the fund's basic law are automatically invalid
  • 基金基本法的修改，必须经过远超普通立法的极高门槛
  • Amendments to the fund's basic law must pass an extremely high threshold far beyond ordinary legislation
  • 任何行政首脑，均无权以行政命令的方式修改、冻结或废除基金基本法的任何条款
  • No chief executive has the power to amend, freeze, or repeal any provision of the fund's basic law by executive order

为什么必须是宪法性法律？

Why must it be constitutional law?

历史证明，行政命令可以被下一道行政命令撤销，普通法律可以被足够大的政治力量修改，而宪法性法律，尽管并非完全不可改变，其修改门槛之高，足以让任何想要动摇基金独立性的人或集团，必须发动一场全面的宪政危机才可能得逞。这个成本，本身就是最强有力的防御机制。

History proves that executive orders can be revoked by the next executive order, ordinary laws can be amended by sufficient political force, but constitutional law—though not completely unchangeable—has such a high amendment threshold that anyone seeking to undermine the fund's independence must launch a full‑scale constitutional crisis. This cost is itself the most powerful defence.

3.3 完整剥离 3.3 Complete Severance

完整剥离是三条原则中最具操作刚性的一条。它要求：一旦资产注入基金，原所有者在法律上、事实上，都不再保留任何形式的所有权、控制权、受益权或追索权。

Complete severance is the most operationally rigid of the three principles. It requires: once assets are injected into the fund, the original owner retains no form of ownership, control, beneficiary rights, or recourse, legally or de facto.

第一重：财产权的完整剥离

First: Complete severance of property rights

  • 划入基金的资产，其所有权在法律上完全转移至基金法人名下。
  • The ownership of assets transferred to the fund is legally and completely transferred to the fund's legal entity.
  • 转移完成后，原所有者不再保留任何追索权、受益权、抵押权、质押权。
  • After transfer, the original owner retains no recourse, beneficiary, mortgage, or pledge rights.
  • 资产的清单、评估报告、划转协议，必须经由国际独立审计机构确认，并作为基金基本法的附件永久存档。
  • The asset inventory, assessment report, and transfer agreement must be confirmed by independent international auditors and permanently archived as attachments to the fund's basic law.
  • 这不是"委托管理"，不是"授权经营"，不是"资产代持"，是所有权的彻底让渡。
  • This is not "entrusted management," not "authorized operation," not "nominal asset holding"—it is complete transfer of ownership.

第二重：人事权的完整剥离

Second: Complete severance of personnel rights

  • 基金的最高决策机构，董事会或理事会，的任何成员，不得由以下人员担任或提名：在职或离任的创始国公职人员、创始国任何政党的高级官员、创始国任何与垄断行业有直接利益关联的人员。
  • No member of the fund's highest decision‑making body—board of directors or council—may be appointed or nominated by: current or former public officials of the founding state, senior officials of any political party in the founding state, or persons with direct interest in monopoly industries of the founding state.
  • 董事会成员的遴选，由完全独立的提名委员会负责。该委员会由退休国际法官、知名学者、国际组织前任高级官员、全球顶级专业机构的代表组成。
  • The selection of board members is the responsibility of a fully independent nomination committee, composed of retired international judges, renowned scholars, former senior officials of international organizations, and representatives of top‑tier global professional institutions.
  • 董事会成员一旦任命，不得被创始国任何机构以行政命令方式解职。解职程序必须启动独立司法审查。
  • Once appointed, board members may not be removed by any institution of the founding state through executive order. Removal procedures must initiate independent judicial review.
  • 国家元首或政府首脑及创始国政府在本制度中的法定角色是：立法监督者。他们可以监督基金是否遵守章程和法律，但不得干预基金的投资决策和日常运营。
  • The legal role of the head of state or government and the founding state's government in this system is: legislative overseer. They may supervise whether the fund complies with the charter and laws, but may not interfere with the fund's investment decisions and daily operations.

第三重：受益权的完整剥离并固化

Third: Complete severance and solidification of beneficiary rights

  • 受益人的范围，全体国民及合法参与主体，和按出资比例获取收益的规则，必须写入基金基本法的正文。
  • The scope of beneficiaries—all citizens and legitimate participants—and the rules for receiving returns in proportion to contributed capital must be written into the body of the fund's basic law.
  • 任何受益人不得因政治立场、宗教信仰、刑事犯罪，除非涉及危害基金本身的罪行，而被剥夺其受益权。受益权是民事权利，与政治权利和人身自由权利完全分离。
  • No beneficiary may be deprived of beneficiary rights due to political stance, religious belief, or criminal conviction—unless the crime endangers the fund itself. Beneficiary rights are civil rights, completely separate from political rights and personal freedom.
  • 新增受益层级或改变分配比例，必须触发修法程序，即达到宪法性法律的修改门槛，而非董事会或某个行政部门可以擅自决定。
  • Adding new beneficiary tiers or changing distribution proportions must trigger the amendment procedure, i.e., meeting the constitutional amendment threshold, and cannot be arbitrarily decided by the board or any administrative department.

3.4 物理隔绝 3.4 Physical Isolation

这是本方案区别于人类历史上所有类似尝试的最关键设计。单独立法和完整剥离解决的是"法律上的独立"，但法律可以被修改，可以被突破。必须有物理层面的不可触碰性，才能让制度真正脱离权力的辐射范围。

This is the most critical design that distinguishes this proposal from all similar attempts in human history. Independent legislation and complete severance address "legal independence," but laws can be amended and breached. There must be physical intangibility for the system to truly escape the reach of power.

第一层：资本与母体的物理隔绝

Layer 1: Physical isolation of capital from the parent

  • 基金的法定注册地，必须选择一个法治高度成熟、司法高度独立、财产权受宪法绝对保护的国际金融中心。基金受注册地法律管辖。
  • The fund's legal place of registration must be an international financial center with a highly mature rule of law, an independent judiciary, and absolute constitutional protection of property rights. The fund is governed by the law of its place of registration.
  • 基金的全部资产，必须交由全球顶级的、与创始国没有利益关联的独立托管银行进行分散托管。托管账户开设在多个国际金融中心，形成法律和地理上的多重防护。
  • All fund assets must be placed with globally top‑tier independent custodian banks with no interest connection to the founding state, spread across multiple international financial centers to form multiple layers of legal and geographical protection.
  • 创始国政府、司法机构或任何个人，对托管账户内的资产不具有冻结权、查询权，除非符合国际司法互助协议的极其严格的触发条件，和划拨权。
  • The founding state's government, judiciary, or any individual has no freeze, query—unless extremely strict conditions under international mutual legal assistance treaties are met—or transfer rights over assets in custodian accounts.

这一设计的直接结果是：即使创始国发生政变、政府更迭、甚至战争，基金的资产在法律和物理上都不在暴力可触及的范围之内。

The direct result of this design: even if the founding state experiences a coup, regime change, or even war, the fund's assets remain legally and physically beyond the reach of violence.

第二层：治理与权力的物理隔绝

Layer 2: Physical isolation of governance from power

  • 基金不受创始国任何机构或个人的指令约束。基金章程中明确规定：任何决策层或管理层成员，如果接受或执行来自创始国方面的投资指令，即构成违反受托人责任，将在注册地法院面临民事诉讼，并在全球范围内承担个人法律责任。
  • The fund is not subject to the directives of any institution or individual of the founding state. The fund's charter explicitly stipulates: any decision‑maker or manager who accepts or executes investment instructions from the founding state violates fiduciary duty and will face civil litigation in the court of the place of registration, as well as personal legal liability globally.
  • 基金的投资决策、调仓计划、持仓明细等内部信息，在法律上属于基金自身的商业秘密，不必、也不应向创始国政府或国家元首进行汇报。信息披露的唯一对象是受益人，全体受益账户持有者，和注册地的法定监管机构。
  • The fund's investment decisions, rebalancing plans, holdings details, and other internal information are legally the fund's own trade secrets, and need not and shall not be reported to the founding state's government or head of state. The only recipients of information disclosure are the beneficiaries—all account holders—and the legal regulatory authorities of the place of registration.
  • 基金与创始国之间的信息通道，仅限于单向：基金向受益人公布经过审计的年度报告和分红信息。不存在反向的"指令通道"。
  • Information channels between the fund and the founding state are limited to one direction: the fund publishes audited annual reports and dividend information to beneficiaries. There is no reverse "instruction channel."

第三层：分配与身份的物理隔绝

Layer 3: Physical isolation of distribution from identity

  • 基金的受益权分配，不经过任何行政中间环节。每个国民的数字身份，通过区块链或同等安全级别的技术手段，直接与基金的分配系统绑定。分红自动到账，不经由任何政府部门审批、拨付或代发。
  • The fund's beneficiary distributions do not pass through any administrative intermediaries. Each citizen's digital identity is directly bound to the fund's distribution system through blockchain or equivalent security technologies. Dividends are automatically credited without any government department's approval, allocation, or disbursement.
  • 分红规则，发放时间、金额计算方式、调整机制，完全由基金的章程和算法自动执行，任何个人或机构不拥有"调整本期分红"的自由裁量权。
  • Dividend rules—distribution timing, calculation methods, adjustment mechanisms—are fully executed automatically by the fund's charter and algorithms. No individual or institution has discretionary power to "adjust this period's dividend."
  • 个人级受益人的受益资格，与其政治态度、选举投票、言论立场、甚至刑事处罚状态完全无关。一个因普通犯罪正在服刑的个人，其受益权不受任何影响。一个公开批评政府的国民，下个月的分红依然准时到账。
  • Individual beneficiaries' eligibility is completely unrelated to their political attitudes, voting, speech positions, or even criminal punishment status. An individual serving a sentence for an ordinary crime remains unaffected. A citizen who openly criticizes the government will still receive their dividend on time next month.
